# Changelog

## v1.0.0 — 2026-10-23
Version described in the manuscript submitted to *Discover Civil Engineering*.
- Axisymmetric Q9 finite-element solver with Boussinesq verification and mesh-convergence checks.
- Witczak moduli for 12 monthly Chengdu temperature states; 132-solution response database.
- MEPDG fatigue and rutting, Asphalt Institute subgrade criterion, Miner's rule with traffic growth.
- Annualised A1–A3 carbon, 40-year schedules, break-even production GWP.
- One-at-a-time sensitivity, channel decomposition, Monte Carlo (n = 1,500) with Spearman ranks.
- Automated tests and continuous integration.
